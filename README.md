# ppt_viewer
Had troubles with expired Microsoft office license and upload ppts to view in the web-view version was honestly painstaking, hence vibe-coded a ppt viewer that helps me view my ppts whenever i wish to.

uses your device's powerpoints's ppt engine to parse the ppts and then runs it on itself.
guide it to ur folder and it will auto-scan and detect the ppts that are present there.
You can view, present, zoom in(900x) and out(25x) and smoothly traverse along. You can also export your ppt as a pdf via it.
